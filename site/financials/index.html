Interim periods and fiscal 2026

The interim financial statements and MD&A for the nine months ended 31 May 2026 are filed on SEDAR+ and listed below. At every reporting date the balance sheet shows a small inventory balance and a matching floorplan balance: the vehicles bought against approved customers and awaiting delivery on that day, and the purchase line that funded them. Days of vehicles, not months. That is what zero standing inventory looks like on a balance sheet.

One line on a number you may have seen elsewhere

Figures for fiscal 2025 circulated before the audit was complete and differ from the audited figure. The audited figure is $6.48 million. Everything OCAL writes uses the audited figure.

Cash, and the going-concern note

OCAL is at an early stage as a public company. Its financial statements, audited and interim, carry a going-concern note, which is common for Venture-listed companies at this stage: it says the Company needs volume or capital to fund its plan. The July 2026 financing raised $1.5 million. Management's plan assumes growth capital in fiscal 2027; no terms have been set, and nothing on this site is an offer of securities. The operating metrics are published every period so that progress towards profitability is visible rather than asserted. Cash at each reporting date is in the filed statements linked below; OCAL does not publish cash figures between reporting dates.

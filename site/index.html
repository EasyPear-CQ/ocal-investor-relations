TSXV: OCAL · FSE: JC3 · INVESTOR RELATIONS

We solve the problem first. The vehicle follows.

OCAL Financial is a technology-enabled automotive financing and purchasing platform. We redesigned the entire vehicle purchasing process around the customer's financial eligibility, needs and convenience: approved by a lender first, then the one vehicle that approval supports, sourced through OPENLANE and delivered to the driveway anywhere in the province. Zero standing inventory, no loans on our books, and audited statements on the public record.

WHY INVESTORS LEAN IN
01

The problems OCAL solves

Some people cannot get approved where they shop. Some cannot find the right vehicle at a payment they can carry. Some live hours from the nearest lot. Some have never had financing explained to them. OCAL solves each of these, in that order, and every one solved is a funded transaction.

Read more02

Proven commercial traction

More than 1,500 funded vehicle transactions since 2021 and audited revenue of $6.48 million in fiscal 2025, up 19 percent, from two provinces with no growth capital. OCAL generates revenue today; it is not developing a product.

Read more03

Operating leverage

The cost base is largely fixed: licences, people, systems and an office that do not grow with the count. In fiscal 2025 revenue rose 18.8 percent and gross profit 32.7 percent while operating expenses fell 9.8 percent, and the operating loss halved. As volume increases the Company has the potential to spread its fixed costs across more vehicle sales. The number that moves is the count.

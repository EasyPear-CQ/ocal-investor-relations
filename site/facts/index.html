Standard description for third parties

Short (one line). Technology-enabled automotive financing and purchasing platform, licensed in British Columbia and Alberta.

Standard (for data vendors). OCAL Financial Inc. (TSXV: OCAL; FSE: JC3) is a technology-enabled automotive financing and purchasing platform transforming how Canadians finance and purchase vehicles. Built around an approval-first approach, OCAL connects customers with suitable financing options before sourcing a vehicle that meets their needs and budget. Its integrated platform brings together credit assessment and financial guidance, lender matching, vehicle sourcing, digital transactions and home delivery anywhere in the provinces it serves, eliminating the need to visit a traditional dealership. OCAL holds no standing vehicle inventory; it sources each vehicle only after a customer is approved, through OPENLANE Canada, its auction and sourcing channel. OCAL earns revenue from vehicle sales and related finance and protection products, and does not hold consumer loans or assume credit-default risk. Licensed in British Columbia and Alberta, OCAL generated revenue of $6.48 million (audited) in the fiscal year ended August 31, 2025 and has completed more than 1,500 funded vehicle transactions to date.

A funded vehicle transaction is a retail vehicle sale arranged through OCAL's platform for which the customer's financing contract was funded by a lender and the vehicle was delivered to the customer. The cumulative count is drawn from the Company's transaction records and lender reporting since its first funded transaction in 2021 and is not a measure presented in its financial statements.

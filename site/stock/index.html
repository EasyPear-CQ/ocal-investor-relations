Escrow

23,638,104 shares, about 64 percent of the shares outstanding, are in escrow. The principals' shares are held in TSXV Tier 2 value escrow: 10 percent was released at listing on 21 July 2026 and 15 percent is released every six months after that, over 36 months. The legacy capital-pool shares sit in CPC escrow released over 18 months. The 6,029,500 shares issued in the July 2026 financing become free trading on 8 November 2026, when the standard four-month hold expires. We publish the schedule so that every investor knows when shares become tradable.

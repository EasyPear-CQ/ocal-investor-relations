NEWS RELEASE · 3 NOVEMBER 2026 · VANCOUVER, BRITISH COLUMBIA

OCAL Financial Reports a Historical Repossession Rate Under One Percent, Highlighting the Strength of Its Asset-Light Automotive Financing Platform, and Announces Meeting Date for Its 2026 Annual General and Special Meeting

Fewer than one in a hundred vehicle loans arranged since 2021 has ended in repossession; approximately 99 percent of signed contracts have been funded by lenders; annual general and special meeting on December 10, 2026.

Vancouver, British Columbia (Newsfile Corp., November 3, 2026). OCAL Financial Inc. (TSXV: OCAL) (FSE: JC3) ("OCAL" or the "Company"), the technology-enabled automotive financing and purchasing platform that gets Canadians approved for financing first and holds no standing vehicle inventory, today announced that fewer than one percent of the vehicle loans arranged through its platform since 2021 have resulted in repossession, according to management information for the period from the Company's first funded transaction in 2021 to September 30, 2026, based on lender reporting.

The Company believes this historical performance reflects the effectiveness of its credit verification procedures, lender relationships, disciplined financing practices, and customer-first approach that prioritizes financial eligibility and affordability before vehicle selection.

With more than 1,500 cumulative funded vehicle transactions and audited revenue of $6.48 million in the fiscal year ended August 31, 2025, OCAL has an established commercial operating history while maintaining an asset-light structure that eliminates the need for standing vehicle inventory. A funded vehicle transaction is a retail vehicle sale arranged through OCAL's platform for which the customer's financing contract was funded by a lender and the vehicle was delivered to the customer; the cumulative count is drawn from the Company's transaction records and lender reporting since its first funded transaction in 2021 and is not a measure presented in its financial statements.

Operating Highlights

Disciplined Transaction Quality and a Scalable Business Model: OCAL's process begins with financing approval and ends with the vehicle, reversing the conventional dealership sequence. Through its approval-first approach, OCAL verifies each customer's identity, income, creditworthiness and financing eligibility, explains the approval in plain terms, and only then sources a vehicle that fits the approved terms. This approach is designed to improve transaction quality, reduce financing uncertainty and strengthen lender confidence.

According to management, approximately 99 percent of signed customer contracts were successfully funded by lenders over the period from the Company's first funded transaction in 2021 to September 30, 2026, reflecting the Company's ability to convert approved transactions into completed vehicle sales. Unlike a lender, OCAL does not retain consumer loans on its balance sheet or assume the associated credit-default risk.

Commercial Traction and Operating Efficiency: OCAL's revenue model combines vehicle sales with financing-related compensation and protection products, creating multiple sources of revenue on every transaction. Management reports that approximately 98 percent of funded transactions include a warranty or other protection product, and that repeat and referral customers accounted for approximately 17.6 percent of funded transactions over the previously disclosed trailing twelve-month period. Every vehicle OCAL sells is under five years old, inspected and reconditioned before delivery, and carries a two-year, unlimited-kilometre warranty.

Technology-Enabled Compliance and Scalable Infrastructure: OCAL operates through a centralized digital workflow that integrates customer verification, financing approvals, vehicle sourcing through OPENLANE Canada after approval, digital contracting and delivery to the customer's door, with every disclosure, signature and transaction recorded. The Company is licensed in British Columbia and Alberta and intends to apply the same infrastructure as it expands into additional Canadian markets, subject to applicable licensing and regulatory approvals.

Management Commentary

Maintaining a repossession rate below one percent over our operating history reflects the discipline behind every transaction and the trust we work to build with our customers.

Mehdi Moghareh, Chief Executive Officer

Operating Performance

Transaction Economics and Path to Profitability

OCAL's asset-light business model is designed to generate multiple revenue streams from each funded vehicle transaction while maintaining a largely fixed operating cost structure. In the fiscal year ended August 31, 2025, the Company's audited revenue increased 18.8 percent to $6,480,254, gross profit increased 32.7 percent to $810,751, or 12.5 percent of revenue (fiscal 2024: $610,787, or 11.2 percent), and operating expenses decreased 9.8 percent to $1,114,195, narrowing the operating loss to $303,444 from $624,056. Finance, insurance and other products contributed $701,727 of fiscal 2025 gross profit.

The Company's operating costs are largely fixed. As transaction volumes increase, OCAL has the potential to spread its fixed costs across more vehicle sales, improving operating efficiency and advancing toward profitability. Beginning with its November 10, 2026 news release, OCAL intends to report operating metrics for each closed month, using the same definitions each time.

The fiscal 2025 and fiscal 2024 figures above are taken from the Company's audited financial statements for the year ended August 31, 2025, filed on SEDAR+ at www.sedarplus.ca.

About OCAL

OCAL Financial Inc. (TSXV: OCAL) (FSE: JC3) is a technology-enabled automotive financing and purchasing platform transforming how Canadians finance and purchase vehicles. Built around an approval-first approach, OCAL connects customers with suitable financing options before sourcing a vehicle that meets their needs and budget. Its integrated platform brings together credit assessment and financial guidance, lender matching, vehicle sourcing, digital transactions and home delivery anywhere in the provinces it serves, eliminating the need to visit a traditional dealership. OCAL holds no standing vehicle inventory; it sources each vehicle only after a customer is approved, through OPENLANE Canada, its auction and sourcing channel. OCAL earns revenue from vehicle sales and related finance and protection products, and does not hold consumer loans or assume credit-default risk. Licensed in British Columbia and Alberta, OCAL generated revenue of $6.48 million (audited) in the fiscal year ended August 31, 2025 and has completed more than 1,500 funded vehicle transactions to date.

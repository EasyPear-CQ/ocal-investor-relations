In sixty seconds

OCAL Financial (TSXV: OCAL, FSE: JC3) is a technology-enabled automotive financing and purchasing platform. It solves the customer's problem first: approval by a lender, then the one vehicle that approval supports, bought at wholesale through OPENLANE, OCAL's auction and sourcing channel, and delivered to the driveway. Zero standing inventory, no consumer loans held, the licensed motor dealer of record on every transaction. Audited fiscal 2025 revenue of $6.48 million, up 18.8 percent, with gross profit up 32.7 percent and operating expenses down 9.8 percent; more than 1,500 funded vehicle transactions since 2021; a largely fixed cost base that does not grow with the count; licensed in British Columbia and Alberta, with the Manitoba dealer permit application under way and Ontario registration under way, targeted for 2027, subject to registration and capital. The founders hold about 54 percent and have not sold a share.

A funded vehicle transaction is a retail vehicle sale arranged through OCAL's platform for which the customer's financing contract was funded by a lender and the vehicle was delivered to the customer. The cumulative count is drawn from the Company's transaction records and lender reporting since its first funded transaction in 2021 and is not a measure presented in its financial statements.

Annual general meeting

The 2026 annual general and special meeting of shareholders will be held on 10 December 2026 at 9:00 am Vancouver time at Suite 1600, 925 West Georgia Street, Vancouver. The record date is 2 November 2026. Shareholders will be asked to set the number of directors at four, elect the directors, approve a new omnibus equity incentive plan to replace the existing stock option plan, and reappoint DMCL LLP as auditor. The management information circular and form of proxy will be sent to shareholders and filed on SEDAR+ before the meeting; the voting results are posted here and filed after it. Questions sent in advance to investors@ocal.ca are answered in writing on this page after the meeting.

Insider ownership

The two founders hold about 54.3 percent of the shares outstanding (Matthew Friesen 14,212,381 shares and Mehdi Moghareh 5,710,825 shares per SEDI, October 2026), in TSXV Tier 2 value escrow released over 36 months from listing. Directors and officers file their holdings and trades on SEDI within five days; the founders have not sold a share since listing. The full table of insider holdings will be in the management information circular for the annual general and special meeting, filed on SEDAR+.
